Generated by `scripts/gen_selector_paper_matrix.py` (its docstring gives the order and the arms). Datasets proteins, asd, synthie, bbbp, aids, colors-3, bzr, enzymes with the first paper's oracle, the margin oracle (τ = 0.9, also tri-40) and the robust one (edge perturbations {'drop': 0.1, 'add': 0.1}, not asd). colors-3 on 20 fixed fold-0 instances. bbbp's original-oracle fold 0 runs locally. Queue A: collections of folds 1 and 2, bls_v0 and bls_v1 on fold 0; then the pretraining of the checkpoints on a compute node; then queue B: selector_v1 on fold 0. ```
